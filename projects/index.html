02
Geospatial ML · Live
California Wildfire Risk & Spread Simulation
Butte County, CA · Unofficial portfolio project — modelled demonstrations, not a source of real fire information
An end-to-end geospatial data science project: a machine-learning ignition-risk model over a 1 km grid of Butte County, a cellular-automaton spread simulation calibrated against the 2018 Camp Fire, and a live NASA FIRMS thermal-detection layer refreshed every 15 minutes by GitHub Actions. Ignition risk scores a PR-AUC of 0.226 under spatially blocked cross-validation against a 0.048 baseline; the spread model reaches an IoU of 0.595 against the observed Camp Fire extent with 11.1 h arrival-time error. Ingests TIGER, FRAP, FPA-FOD, LANDFIRE, gridMET, HRRR, and FIRMS, with circular variables — wind, aspect, season — stored as vectors so every aggregation is correct by construction. Free infrastructure throughout.

03
Data Platform · Live
Nuclide Explorer
An interactive chart of every known nuclide — 3,386 of them — built on the IAEA Livechart API over ENSDF. Protons up, neutrons across, coloured by decay mode or half-life; click any nuclide to walk its decay chain to stability. A Python ingestion pipeline and layered dbt models on Neon Postgres reconcile the quirks of the source data — half-lives published as bounds, units that mix time with energy, wide decay columns that the graph needs tall — into a typed schema, verified against IAEA's own values to a relative tolerance of 1e-6. Rendered on Canvas 2D with d3-zoom and served through a Zod-validated, rate-limited public JSON API.
